How a project runs

Five steps from first call to launch

You see working progress every week, so there are no surprises on delivery day.

  1. Brief

    A free call to understand your goals. You get a written scope, timeline and quote.

  2. Design

    Wireframes and a visual direction for your approval before any code is written.

  3. Build

    Front-end and back-end are built together, with a demo link shared every week.

  4. Launch

    Domain, HTTPS, hosting and a final check on phones, tablets and desktops.

  5. Support

    Bug fixes after launch, plus optional monthly maintenance as you grow.
